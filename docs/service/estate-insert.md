## The wallet's map is also on Bitcoin

descriptor together, and the keys you still hold cannot rebuild the wallet,
even when they are enough to sign. An encrypted copy of that descriptor is
on the Bitcoin blockchain. Nobody can delete it and nobody can lose it.
