## Tirohanga Whānui {#overview}

He pouaka haumaru matihiko a Kaitiaki, he maha ōna kī. Māu e whiriwhiri ko wai mā ka pupuri, tokohia hoki me huihui kia puare ai.

Kei raro iho, ka whakamunatia ō kōnae ki [age](https://github.com/FiloSottile/age), ka wāwāhia te kī ki waenga i ngā tāngata e whakaponohia ana e koe, ā, ka hoatu ki ia tangata tētahi paihere motuhake mō te whakaora.

Ko te whakaora, he huaki noa i tētahi kōnae ki te pūtirotiro.\* Kāore he tūmau, kāore hoki e hiahiatia kia ora tonu tēnei paetukutuku.

<p style="font-size: 0.8125rem; color: #8A8480;">* Ko ngā pūranga <a href="#timelock" style="color: #8A8480;">raka-ā-wā</a> me hono poto ki te ipurangi i te wā whakaora.</p>

<div class="tip">
<strong>Tohutohu:</strong> Kāore e taea e te tangata kotahi te uru ki ō raraunga. Me huihui rātou i ngā wāhanga e rahi ana, hei tauira 3 o te 5.
</div>

## He aha ai a Kaitiaki {#why-rememory}

Tērā pea kei a koe ētahi mea huna matihiko e whai tikanga ana: ngā waehere whakaora o te pūwhakahaere kupuhipa, ngā tuhinga nui, ngā tohutohu mā te whānau. Ka ahatia ēnei ki te ngaro ohorere koe?

Whakaarohia he pouaka penapena e rua ōna kī kia puare ai. Kāore he tangata kotahi e whai kī rahi hei uru ko ia anake.

He ngoikoretanga ō ngā huarahi tawhito:

- **Hoatu te katoa ki te tangata kotahi**: kotahi noa te tūnga hinga, kotahi noa te whakapono
- **Wāwāhi ā-ringa i ngā kōnae**: he raruraru, he hapa noa, kāore he whakamunatanga
- **Whakamahi i te urunga ohotata a te pūwhakahaere kupuhipa**: he rite ki te "hoatu te katoa ki te tangata kotahi", ka whakawhirinaki anō ki te oranga tonutanga o te kamupene
- **Tuhi ki te wira**: ka noho hei rēkoata tūmatanui, he pōturi te ara ture

He rerekē te huarahi a Kaitiaki:

- **Kāore he tūnga hinga kotahi**: me mahi tahi te tokomaha
- **Kāore e whakawhirinaki ki te tangata kotahi**: ahakoa tō kaitiaki pono rawa, kāore e taea e ia anake te uru ki ō mea huna
- **He tuimotu, he motuhake**: ka ora te whakaora me te kore ipurangi, me te kore tūmau\*
- **I hangaia mā te katoa**: he tohutohu mārama, ehara i te panga whakamuna

## Te Hanga Paihere {#creating}

E toru ngā hipa. Ka mahia katoatia ki tō pūtirotiro. Kāore ō kōnae e wehe atu i tō pūrere. Huakina te [kaihanga paihere](maker.html) hei tīmata.

### Te Tuari ki ngā Kaitiaki {#distributing}

Hoatu ki ia kaitiaki tana paihere mā te ara tūmataiti rawa e taea ana e koe, ko tēnei te raupapa pai:

1. **Ā-kanohi:** He puku USB, he PDF kua tāia rānei, ka hoatu ā-ringa. Ko te ara tūmataiti rawa tēnei.
1. **Karere whakamuna:** Ko Signal, tētahi atu kaikawe karere whakamuna-mutunga-ki-te-mutunga rānei.
1. **Īmēra noa, kāpata kapua rānei:** Kāore e tūtohutia mō ngā taonga tairongo. Ka puritia he kape e te kaiwhakarato, ā, ka noho tō pūkete hei wāhi kohikohi.

Ki te tukuna ngā paihere mā te īmēra, mā te karere rānei, mukua ngā karere i tō kōpaki tuku i muri i te whakaū a ia kaitiaki kua tae mai. Ki te kore, ka mau tonu i tō pūkete he kape o ia wāhanga, ā, ka kore noa te take o te wāwāhi.

### I Muri i te Hanga Paihere {#after-creating}

Kia rite ō paihere, tērā ētahi mahi pai i mua i tō wareware ki tēnei:

- Whakaūhia kua tae te paihere ki ia tangata, ā, ka taea e ia te huaki i `recover.html`
- Kōrerohia ki ia tangata he aha tēnei, he aha i a ia ai, me tiaki hoki e ia. Kāore e taea e ia anake. Me mahi tahi ia ki ētahi atu.
- Puritia he kape o `MANIFEST.age` ki tētahi wāhi haumaru. He raraunga whakamuna noa, he kore noa iho me te kore wāhanga e rahi ana
- Tiakina tō `project.yml` kia taea ai e koe te whakaputa anō i ngā paihere ā tōna wā
- Tāia a `README.pdf` hei pūrua pepa i mua i te tuku i te paihere matihiko. Kāore te pepa e hiahia urutau, hiko rānei.
- Whakaritea he whakamaharatanga ā-tau hei toro atu. Tirohia [Te Pupuri i ngā Paihere kia Hou](#keeping-current)
- Whakaarohia te whakahaere i tētahi [whakaharatau whakaora](#recovery-drills). Koinā anake te ara e mōhio ai koe e ora ana tō mahere

## Ngā Tikanga Pai {#best-practices}

### Te Whiriwhiri Kaitiaki

- **Te roa o te wā:** He tāngata ka taea pea te whakapā atu ā ngā tau 5–10
- **Te marara ā-whenua:** Kaua rātou katoa e noho ki te wāhi kotahi
- **Te pūkenga hangarau:** He pai ngā momo katoa. I hangaia te taputapu mā te katoa
- **Ngā hononga:** Ka mahi tahi rātou tētahi ki tētahi?
- **Te whakapono:** Kāore he mea e whākina e te wāhanga kotahi, engari e tuku ana koe i te haepapa ki a rātou

### Ngā Whakaaro Haumarutanga

- Kaua e puritia ngātahitia ngā paihere katoa. Ka kore noa te take o te wāwāhi
- Whakaarohia te tā i a `README.pdf`. Ka ora te pepa i ngā aituā matihiko
- Tiakina a `project.yml` mēnā e hiahia ana koe ki te whakaputa anō i ngā paihere ā tōna wā

### Te Pupuri Haumaru i ngā Paihere {#storing-bundles}

He iti ngā paihere (iti iho i te 10 MB), i hangaia kia puritia ki ngā wāhi noa. Anei ngā mea e pai ana:

- **Te īmēra** he kōwhiringa tino pai. He maha ngā tāngata ka pupuri i te wāhitau īmēra kotahi mō ngā tekau tau, ā, he iti rawa ngā paihere hei tāpiri atu. He maha ngā kaiwhakarato īmēra ka pupuri i ngā karere mō ake tonu.
- **Te rokiroki kapua** (Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud) he pai hei kape tuarua.
- **Ngā puku USB** ka pai pea, engari kia mahara ka panoni ngā hononga i te wā (kua whakakapia haere a USB-A e USB-C), ā, ka memeha pea te mahara flash ki te noho hikokore mō ngā tau. Ehara i te mea pai hei kape kotahi anake.
- **Te pepa** te mea mauroa rawa. Mā te tā i a `README.pdf` ka whai kape ō kaitiaki kāore e hiahia urutau, hiko, pūrere ora rānei.

Ko te huarahi pai rawa ko te tāpuhipuhi: he īmēra me te pepa, he kapua me te pepa rānei. Nui ake i te kape kotahi, nui ake i te momo kotahi.

### Te Pupuri i ngā Paihere kia Hou {#keeping-current}

Whakaritea he whakamaharatanga ā-tau hei toro atu ki ō kaitiaki. Whakaūhia kei a rātou tonu ā rātou paihere, whakahoutia ngā taipitopito whakapā ki te panoni tētahi mea.

Ka panoni ō kōnae, hangaia he paihere hou, tukuna atu. Kāore ngā paihere tawhito e huaki i te pūranga hou, nō reira kāore he mōrea o te waiho noa. Heoi, tonoa ngā kaitiaki kia whakakapia ā rātou kia mā ai ngā mea katoa.

Ka panoni ngā whakapā, hei tauira ka neke tētahi, e hiahia ana rānei koe ki te tāpiri, ki te tango rānei i tētahi, pēnā anō: he paihere hou, tonoa ngā tāngata kia mukua ngā mea tawhito.

I waenga i ngā whakahoutanga, puritia ō kōnae pūtake ki tētahi pouaka whakamuna. He pai ngā taputapu pēnei i a [Cryptomator](https://cryptomator.org), i a [VeraCrypt](https://veracrypt.fr). Kaua e waiho ngā kape kupu-mārama ki tētahi kōpaki noa.

Whakaarohia he rite ki te whakahou i ō whakapā ohotata. He poto, he auau, he mea pai.

### Ngā Whakaharatau Whakaora {#recovery-drills}

Kāore he tangata e whakamātau i tana mahere pūrua. Mā te whakaharatau whakaora e panoni tērā, ā, he rite tonu ki tētahi rūma mawhiti.

Hangaia he paihere whakamātau me tētahi mea huna kino-kore (he whakaahua, he karere, he tohutaka). Tuaria ngā wāhanga ki ō kaitiaki. Whakaritea he [raka-ā-wā](#timelock) ki te hiahia koe i tētahi tatauriki tūturu. Kātahi ka tū atu, waiho mā rātou e rapu: te mahi tahi, te whakakotahi wāhanga, te wetewete i ngā kōnae me tō kore āwhina.

Ka ako koe:

- Ka kitea rānei e ō kaitiaki ā rātou paihere ina hiahiatia?
- Ka mārama rātou ki ngā tohutohu, ka raru rānei?
- Ka taea e rātou te whakapā tētahi ki tētahi me tō kore whakarite?

Ki te pai te haere o te whakaharatau, ka pai anō tō mahere tūturu. Ki te kino te haere o te whakaharatau, he koha tērā: kua kitea e koe te raruraru i mua i te wā nui.

### Te Whakakore Urunga {#revoking-access}

Kia tuaria tētahi wāhanga, kāore e taea te whakakore. He mea whakarite tēnei. Kāore he tūmau, kāore he mana matua.

Ki te hiahia koe ki te panoni ko wai mā e pupuri ana i ngā wāhanga:

1. **Hangaia he paihere hou** me tētahi rōpū kaitiaki hou, me tētahi kī hou
1. **Tukuna ngā paihere hou** ki ngā kaitiaki e whakaponohia tonutia ana e koe
1. **Tonoa ia kaitiaki e toe ana kia mukua tana paihere tawhito**, kia whakakapia ki te mea hou

<div class="warning">
<strong>He mea nui:</strong> Ka mahi tonu ngā wāhanga tawhito ki ngā pūranga tawhito. Ka tuku koe i tētahi paihere hou, kia mārama te kōrero: <strong>mukua te mea tawhito</strong>, puritia ko te mea hou anake. Kāore he hītori putanga, kāore he "mō te aha noa".
</div>

He pēnā anō ka panoni ngā mea huna. Ko te paihere hou he kī hou, he wāhanga hou. Kāore ngā wāhanga tawhito e huaki i te pūranga hou, engari ka mahi tonu ki te mea tawhito. Kia mōhio koe kāore ngā kaitiaki e pupuri tonu ana i ngā kape tawhito.

### Mō project.yml {#project-file}

Ka hanga koe i ngā paihere, ka tiakina tō kaupapa ki tētahi kōnae `project.yml`. Ka puritia e tēnei kōnae:

- Ngā ingoa me ngā taipitopito whakapā o ngā kaitiaki
- Tō tatau paepae i whiriwhiria (hei tauira, 3 o te 5)
- He hash whakaū hei tirotiro mēnā e hāngai ana ngā paihere
- Ngā tapeke tirotiro wāhanga hei whakaū i te tika o ngā paihere

**Kāore** e puritia ana tētahi mea huna: kāore he kupu huna, kāore he rawa kī, kāore he kaupapa kōnae. He haumaru te pupuri i te taha o ō atu kōnae kaupapa.

Mā `project.yml`, ka taea e koe te whakaputa anō i ngā paihere, te whakaū i ngā mea o nāianei, te tirotiro hoki i te āhua o tō whakaritenga.

## Te Mārama ki te Haumarutanga {#security}

Ka whakakotahi a Kaitiaki i ngā taputapu whakamuna kua whakapūmautia, kāore e hanga i āna ake. Anei te tikanga o tērā.

### Ngā Mea e Tiaki ana i Ō Raraunga {#cryptography}

Ka rakaina ō kōnae ki tētahi taputapu whakamuna hou ([age](https://github.com/FiloSottile/age)), kua arotakengia whānuitia, kāore he ngoikoretanga e mōhiotia ana.

Ko te kī e raka ana i a rātou he 256 ngā bit te roa, i whakaputaina i te pūwhakaputa tau matapōkere o tō pūnaha whakahaere. Hei ine: ka roa ake te matapae i te oranga o te ao tukupū.

Ahakoa ka whakamātauria e tētahi ngā kupuhipa katoa, mā scrypt ka āta whakapōturitia ia matapae, he miriona whakarea te pōturi i te ngana noa.

Kātahi ka wāwāhia te kī mā te Shamir's Secret Sharing. **Ko ngā wāhanga iti iho i te *tatau paepae* kāore he mōhiohio kore noa mō te taketake.** Ehara i te "iti noa". He kore pāngarau.

Kei ia paihere ngā tapeke tirotiro kia taea ai e te taputapu whakaora te whakaū kāore he mea i pirau, i rawekehia rānei.
